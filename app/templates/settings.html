Demo data

This is a demonstration build. Patients, bookings, callbacks, calls, notifications and fees are sample data. No WhatsApp, SMS, phone calls or payments are made, and nothing here is medical advice.

Do not enter real patient information. Only the clinic name is real; the contact details, doctors, hours and fees are placeholders until the clinic owner approves them. Restoring the sample data is done by the person running the demo, outside the app; there is deliberately no reset control on this page.
