How these figures are calculated

Period
“Today” is the current day. “This week” is Monday to Sunday of the current week. “All demo data” is everything stored in the demo.
Appointment funnel
Starts from bookings created in the period, whatever day the appointment itself is on. Confirmed, arrived and completed count bookings that have reached that stage at any point so far. No-shows and cancellations count bookings currently in that state. Shares are a percentage of the bookings created.
Source
Waitlist replacement: the booking was made from a waitlist entry. Voice demo: a simulated call log is linked to the booking. Website AI assistant: made by the assistant with no linked call. Staff: made at reception. Website form (sample) appears only for the seeded sample booking marked as coming from a web form.
Callbacks, calls, waitlist, notifications
Records created (calls: started) in the period, grouped by their current status or outcome. Calls and notifications are simulations.
Arrivals and QR check-in
Arrival and QR check-in events recorded in the period. Arrivals recorded before the patient timeline existed are not included.
Doctor consultations
Outcomes and draft recommendations recorded in the period, counted by fixed category.
Procedure follow-ups
Doctor recommendations created in the period, by their current state (draft, ready for scheduling, scheduled), and follow-up appointments booked in the period. No procedure names or notes are shown. Consultation follow-ups are requests a doctor sent to reception in the period, and how many of those are still waiting or have been scheduled.
What is not here
No revenue, satisfaction, forecast or real conversion figures: the demo does not record them, so none are shown. No patient is identified anywhere on this page.
