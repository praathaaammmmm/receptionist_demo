How entries are created. Confirming a booking queues a confirmation; cancelling queues a cancellation update; a confirmed booking starting within {{ reminder_hours }} hours gets a reminder; marking a waitlist patient as offered queues an offer; a new callback task queues an acknowledgement. Each event produces one entry. Wording comes from the editable templates in app/notification_templates.py and never includes prices or clinical advice.
